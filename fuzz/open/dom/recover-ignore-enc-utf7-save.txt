recover-ignore-enc-utf7-save.bin (dom target, options driver, found 2026-10-19,
315 s run; reproduces on main e01d329ca)

The selector byte 0xfc sets RECOVER, IGNORE_ENC|SKIP_IDS, NOBLANKS, DTDVALID
and NOCDATA|NSCLEAN. The document declares encoding="UTF-7". With IGNORE_ENC
the parse reads it as UTF-8, but the recovered tree keeps the declared label,
so fuzz/options.rs check_recovered's `save::to_vec(tree, &Default::default())`
returns UnsupportedEncoding and its `expect("save")` panics. Decide whether
`save` with no requested encoding should fall back for a label the parse
ignored (what does libxml2 record in doc->encoding under IGNORE_ENC?), or the
harness should save with an explicit UTF-8 encoding.
