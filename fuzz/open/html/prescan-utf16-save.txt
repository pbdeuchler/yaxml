prescan-utf16-save.html (html target, found 2026-10-19 by a hand seed)

A non-ASCII byte without a known encoding triggers the htmlFindEncoding
prescan (src/html.rs Engine::fallback). It accepts a UTF-16 label, so the
document's html_encoding() becomes "utf-16", but html::save / to_html reject
that label (src/html/encoding.rs Output::for_label -> UnsupportedEncoding).
The harness asserts that every successfully parsed document serializes
(fuzz/html.rs `outcome`, "parsed encodings serialize").

Either the fallback should refuse non-ASCII-compatible labels (as `declare`
does for <meta>), or the serializer needs UTF-16 output as libxml2 has.
