laughs-slow-unit.xml (dtd target, 2026-10-19, timeout at -timeout=2)

A billion-laughs DTD within the default expansion limits takes about 2.1 s
through the whole dtd driver in the ASan fuzz build (about 0.16 s in a plain
release build). Expansion is bounded, so this is a slow unit, not a hang;
the dtd target needs -timeout >= 5 or per-surface profiling to find whether
one surface is super-linear in the expanded size.
